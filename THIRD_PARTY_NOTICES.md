# Third-party notices

## Instagram downloader reference

This project reuses/adapts small, general-purpose pieces of the public repository:

https://github.com/riad-azz/instagram-video-downloader

That repository is licensed under the MIT License. Its MIT license text is retained in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` below for attribution purposes.

The reused/adapted ideas include Instagram shortcode parsing, the public-media download-proxy pattern, response/error handling structure, and the general separation between frontend form handling and the server-side Instagram media route.
